Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2026

Summary: MediaData Player does not collect, store, or share any personal data. All operations happen locally on your device.

1. Data Collection

MediaData Player is committed to protecting user privacy. We want to be clear about what data we do and do not collect:

  • We DO NOT collect:
    • Personal information (name, email, phone number)
    • User IDs or device identifiers
    • Location data
    • Browsing history or media consumption patterns
    • Financial information
    • Photos, videos, or media content
    • Contact lists or social network data
  • What happens on your device:
    • All media playback occurs locally
    • File management is done within your device storage
    • Streaming URLs are processed in real-time and not logged
    • Settings and preferences are stored locally using Android SharedPreferences
Content Disclaimer: MediaData Player is a media player tool. It does not provide any media content or streaming services. Users are solely responsible for providing their own content and ensuring they have the legal right to view such content.

2. Local Storage

The app requires access to your device storage solely for:

  • Playing local media files you select
  • Saving network media to user-designated folders (including temporary parts for high-speed multi-threaded downloading)
  • Storing app preferences (subtitle settings, language, etc.)

Important: You have full control over what files the app can access. The app only accesses files you explicitly open or folders you explicitly grant permission to.

3. Internet Connectivity

The app requires internet permission for:

  • Streaming content from user-provided URLs
  • Casting to Google Cast devices
  • Saving network media to local storage

What we DON'T do:

  • We don't send your data to our servers (we don't have servers)
  • We don't track which URLs you access
  • We don't monitor your streaming habits
  • We don't share your activity with third parties

4. Third-Party Services

MediaData Player uses the following Android and Google services:

  • Google Cast: For casting to TV devices. Google Cast may collect device information as per Google's privacy policy
  • PDDownloader: A custom download engine integrated into the app for accelerated, multi-threaded downloading. It operates entirely locally on your device and does not transmit data to external servers.
  • Android DownloadManager: System service for saving files. Managed by Android OS
  • Media3 (ExoPlayer): Google's open-source media playback library. No data collection

No Analytics or Tracking: We do not use:

  • Google Analytics
  • Firebase Analytics
  • Crashlytics or crash reporting
  • Facebook SDK
  • Any advertising SDKs
  • Any third-party tracking services

5. Permissions Explained

Permission Purpose
INTERNET Required for streaming and saving network media
FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK Maintains playback during casting and background operation to ensure uninterrupted audio/video playback when you switch to another app or turn off the screen
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Shows save progress and playback controls
Storage Access Read and write media files (user-granted folders only)

6. Data Security

  • All data remains on your device
  • Network streams use HTTPS when available
  • No cloud storage or external databases
  • No authentication or user accounts

7. Age Requirement and Children's Privacy

MediaData Player is intended for use by individuals who are at least 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect, maintain, or use personal information from children under 13 years of age. If you are under 13, please do not use or access the app at any time or in any manner.

If we learn that personal information of persons under 13 has been collected, we will take the appropriate steps to delete this information. Since the app does not collect user data to our servers, children's privacy is primarily protected by the lack of data collection and the local-only nature of the app.
